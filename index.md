## About Me

<!-- TODO: fill in your current position, lab, and advisor in the sentence below. -->
I am a Ph.D. student working on robot perception and learning. I received my M.S.E. in
Robotics from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/), where I worked
in the [GRASP Laboratory](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) on metric-semantic mapping and
multi-robot SLAM.

My work spans the full stack of embodied autonomy: building semantic maps that let robot teams
understand and navigate unstructured environments, and learning manipulation policies that fuse
vision with touch and force feedback for contact-rich tasks.

## Research Interests

- **Robot Perception and Mapping:** metric-semantic SLAM, multi-robot mapping, spatio-temporal mapping
- **Robot Learning and Manipulation:** contact-rich manipulation, vision-tactile fusion, world models
- **Field Robotics:** autonomous UAVs and UGVs for agriculture and forestry
