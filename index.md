I am a Ph.D. student working on robot learning at Texas A&M University. I received my M.S.E. in
Robotics from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/), where I worked
in the [GRASP Laboratory](https://www.grasp.upenn.edu/) on robot perception and multi-robot system for agricultural applications. I received my bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering from Sichuan University.

## Research Interests

- **Robot Learning and Manipulation:** I build robots that understand and act in the physical world, with a focus on learning policies whose decisions are explainable and controllable.
- **Field Robotics:** robotic mapping and navigation for agriculture and forestry
